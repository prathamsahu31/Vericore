## Presentation Structure Overview

1. **Executive Summary & Problem Context:** Challenges of bid compliance verification in GeM procurement for CPSEs and ministries.
2. **Current Workflow vs. Vericore Workflow:** Transition from manual, multi-day PDF checks to automated, citation-backed evaluation.
3. **Core Architectural Innovation:** 9-layer pipeline, deterministic rule evaluation, and hash-chained audit trails.
4. **Multi-Portal Verification Engine:** Simulated and hybrid adapters for GSTN, PAN, MCA21, Udyam, and Debarment registers.
5. **Two-Gate Evaluation & Officer Empowerment:** How Vericore enforces the principle: *"The officer decides. The system never does."*
6. **Live Demo & Test Case Walkthrough:** Demonstration across Clean, Problematic, and Ambiguous bidder bundles.
7. **Security, Auditability & Legal Defensibility:** SHA-256 hash chains, immutable storage, and role-based access.
8. **Scalability & Future Roadmap:** GeM API integration, multilingual OCR, and federated tender boards.

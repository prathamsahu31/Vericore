checklist for a procurement officer.

Find the pre-qualification / eligibility criteria section and the technical
qualification section. In this kind of document they are numbered sections
containing a table whose columns are typically:

    SN | Basic Criteria | Detailed Criteria | Applicability | Documents Required

Return one entry per table ROW — one per numbered criterion. Do not merge rows,
and do not split one row into several.

Return JSON of exactly this shape:

APPLICABILITY — read the Applicability column, do not assume:
  "Sole Bidder or prime bidder of the Consortium"    -> "lead_only"
  "Sole Bidder or all members in the consortium"     -> "all_members"
  "Sole Bidder and each consortium member"           -> "all_members"
  "Sole Bidder or any consortium member"             -> "any_member"
  combined / aggregate turnover permitted            -> "aggregate"
If the column is absent or you cannot tell, use "lead_only" and lower your
confidence. The stricter reading is the safe error.

Rules for "source_span" — this field is load-bearing, read carefully:

- It must be the text VERBATIM as printed in the document, character for
  character, including its original punctuation, spacing and case.
- It must NOT be the normalised value. If the page prints "31.03.2024" and you
  report a value of "2024-03-31", then source_span is "31.03.2024".
- It must be long enough to be unambiguous on that page, but no longer than the
  value and its immediate label.
- If you cannot quote the text exactly, omit the field entirely rather than
  approximating the span.

The span is used to locate the value's position on the page so a human can be
shown exactly where it came from. An approximate span points a procurement
officer at the wrong part of the page, which is worse than no span at all.

Do not report coordinates, bounding boxes, or pixel positions. You are not able
to observe them, and they are computed separately from your span.
